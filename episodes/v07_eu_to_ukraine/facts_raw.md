# v07 "What you can't carry from EU to Ukraine" - raw facts (research 2026-11-18)
Caveat: customs.gov.ua, zakon.rada, protocol.ua returned HTTP 403 to fetch; numbers below come from secondary media citing Customs Code (MK) art.374-376. Verify against zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/4495-17 before final script. Tags: [SOLID] = 2+ consistent sources/official; [DISPUTED] = single/secondary/unverified.

## 7 chosen items
1. Cash over EUR 10,000
2. Alcohol and tobacco limits
3. Medicines (prescription/narcotic/psychotropic)
4. Food of animal origin (meat/dairy/sausage)
5. Goods above duty-free limit (phones/iPhone) + parcels VAT change
6. Vapes / e-cigs (and "surprise" items: drones, thermal imagers, body armor, cultural artefacts)
7. Pets (chip, rabies 21 days, antibody test, vet certificate)
(Cars/fuel canisters: no verifiable personal-use rule found - skip or treat as [DISPUTED].)

## Item rules
### 1 Cash [SOLID]
- Up to EUR 10,000 equivalent: no written declaration. Above: written customs declaration + documents proving origin (residents: bank payment order/ statement of cash withdrawal). Limit is per person (not per family group) [DISPUTED on family point]. No upper cap on amount.
- Src: https://buhplatforma.com.ua/news/94070-vyvezennia-valiutnykh-tsinnostei-za-mezhi-ukrainy-roziasnennia-vid-derzhmytsluzhby ; https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/12122017_127.pdf (NBU instruction); https://dengi.ua/ua/budget/9763070-vivezennya-gotivki-z-ukrayini-vipiska-z-banku-zaminit-inshi-dokumenti
- Warning: some outdated sites claim non-residents need no docs (2012) - ignore.

### 2 Alcohol/tobacco (18+ only) [DISPUTED-ish: single detailed source, matches known MK art.376]
- Beer 5 L; non-sparkling wine 2 L; spirits (>22%) 1 L; 200 cigarettes (1 block) OR 50 cigars OR 250 g tobacco (mixed total 250 g). Excess: declare and pay duties. Under 18: forbidden.
- Src: https://www.kyivpost.com/uk/amp/post/54737 (cites MK art.376 - confirm exact text).

### 3 Medicines [DISPUTED numbers]
- Personal use: up to 5 packages per name (per kyivpost); narcotic/psychotropic/strong ("сильнодіючі") need prescription or equivalent doc. Src: https://www.kyivpost.com/uk/amp/post/54737
- Customs 2026: 540 drug/psychotropic/precursor cases Jan-May 2026, most on Poland and Moldova borders. Src: https://mezha.net/eng/?p=2791121
- Jan-Feb 2026: 244 cases (178 imports, 66 exports); Poland border 72, Moldova 36; 136 customs protocols, 202 reports to law enforcement with criminal signs; psychotropics 15.42 kg (thousands of pills/capsules/candies), kratom 2.47 kg. Src: https://unn.ua/en/amp/almost-250-cases-in-two-months-customs-records-a-surge-in-attempts-to-import-drugs-into-ukraine
- Not found: specific tramadol/codeine passenger case on Polish border 2025-26. Only: 1,200 "Danabol" steroid tablets hidden in clothes, Rava-Ruska, 2024 (https://medplatforma.com.ua/news/183-u-lvivskijj-oblasti-zatrimano-kontrabandista-shho-viz-partiju-zaboronenikh-steroyidiv). No exact MOZ source fetched.

### 4 Food [SOLID for numbers, DISPUTED for meat ban]
- Food for own use: up to EUR 200 per person; per product name: 1 retail package or <=2 kg; unpackaged <=2 kg per name. Must be declared (verbal/action/written). Basis: Cabinet Resolution No.434 of 21.05.2012. Src: https://dpss.gov.ua/news/vvezennya-harchovih-produktiv-v-ukrayinu-dlya-vlasnogo-spozhivannya-shcho-varto-znati
- Animal-origin products: allowed unless bans after outbreaks (ASF etc.) in the origin country/region; check "Дійові заборони" on DPSS site. Past examples: Hungary (2018) all animal products banned; some Polish voivodeships for pigs. So NOT a blanket EU ban.
- Kyivpost claims "meat and dairy without vet certificates prohibited" - not confirmed by DPSS page [DISPUTED].
- MYTH direction: EU bans personal meat/dairy INTO the EU from third countries (incl. Ukraine) - opposite direction. https://www.qdpro.com.ua/uk/node/36390

### 5 Over-limit goods and parcels [SOLID]
- Duty-free without written declaration: by air EUR 1,000; by land/other EUR 500 AND/OR 50 kg; entering max once per day. Excess typically 10% duty + 20% VAT (kyivpost) [duty rate varies by goods]. Postal: EUR 150 limit. Src: https://www.kyivpost.com/uk/amp/post/54737 ; MK art.374 via https://dspace.uzhnu.edu.ua/jspui/bitstream/lib/34678/1/ (law may have changed)
- Personal items allowed in one unit: 2 phones, 1 laptop, 1 tablet, 1 camera etc (kyivpost) [DISPUTED].
- Parcels 2026 change: Laws No.5025-IX (Tax Code) and 5026-IX (Customs Code), passed 15.10.2026, signed 22.10.2026, in force 23.10.2026 mostly. VAT 20% on distance sales (marketplaces) up to EUR 150 starts 45 days after Cabinet decision, NOT before 1 July 2027. C2C parcels VAT-free to EUR 45 (was 150). Unaccompanied baggage stays VAT-free to EUR 150. 455-day penalty grace after launch. So: today (Nov 2026) EUR 150 parcel VAT exemption still works. Src: https://news.dtkt.ua/taxation/pdv/115880-opodatkuvannia-pdv-posilok-z-za-kordonu-u-2027-roci-analiz-zmin-do-pku-ta-mku-za-zakonami-5025-ta-5026 ; https://buhplatforma.com.ua/news/129788-prezydent-pidpysav-zakony-pro-pdv-na-mizhnarodni-posylky-do-150-ievro ; https://dengi.ua/ua/finance/9763178-zelenskij-pidpisav-zakoni-pro-podatok-na-posilki-ta-dokhodi-v-interneti
- EU side: EUR 3 flat duty per item category on parcels <EUR 150 from 1 July 2026 (applies to EU imports from third countries) - irrelevant to Ukraine entry. https://mezha.net/ua/?p=1118927
- iPhone: customs reported 18 protocols in Sept 2025 for Apple phone attempts; https://thepage.ua/ua/news/skilki-kontrabandi-iphone-17-vilucheno-mitniceyu-ciyeyi-oseni/amp (403, not read - only snippet).

- Drones/thermal imagers/dual-use: need permits; customs lists "dual-use goods" among top postal violations. Src: https://thepublic.info/en/news/smartfoni_liki_ta_kulturni_cinnosti_mitniki_vikrili_sxemi_peresilannia_cerez_postu (not verified on body armor/binoculars rules).

### 7 Pets [SOLID, official]
- Dogs/cats: ISO microchip; rabies vaccine >12 weeks old, primary >=21 days before entry; rabies antibody titre >=0.5 IU/ml, blood taken 3-12 months before dispatch (no retest if vaccination continuous); international vet certificate from departure country authority; >5 animals only for shows/sport, >6 months. Basis: order No.553 of 16.11.2018. Src: https://dpss.gov.ua/mizhnarodne-spivrobitnictv/veterinariya-ta-bezpechnist/vimogi-do-nekomercijnogo-peremishchennya-tvarin/v-ukrainu
- Note: page does not mention EU pet passport; ask DPSS.

## Myths to avoid
- "EUR 150 is duty-free at the land border" - false: EUR 150 is for post; people carry EUR 500/50kg land, EUR 1,000 air.
- "VAT on EUR 150 parcels already started" - no, not before 1 Jul 2027.
- "Red/green corridor / Mobile app changes 2025" - not confirmed; don't claim.
- "Ukraine bans all EU meat/dairy" - no; EU bans it into EU from Ukraine. Ukraine: EUR 200, 2 kg/name, outbreak-based bans.
- "10,000 EUR is banned" - no, only written declaration + proof of origin above it.
- "Unlimited meds" - no; narcotic/psychotropic need prescription.
- Do not state exact fine in UAH or tramadol limits without checking.
