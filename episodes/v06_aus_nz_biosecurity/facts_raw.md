## 1. AUSTRALIA - legal frame
- Biosecurity Act 2015; Incoming Passenger Card (IPC) must declare food, plant material (incl. wooden articles), animal products. "If unsure, declare." Declaring = no fine; may be inspected, cleared, treated or seized. https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/travelling/to-australia [SOLID]
- Airport infringement notice = penalty units (PU). Tiers: 2 PU (min), 6 PU (fresh fruit/veg/herbs etc.), 12 PU (high-risk undeclared goods: meat, dairy), 20 PU (concealed goods, s186A). https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/policy/legislation/compliance/infringement-notice-scheme/infringement-notices-at-the-airport [SOLID]
- PU value changed: A$222 (2022) -> A$275 (1 Jan 2023) -> A$330 (current per airport page: 2PU=$660, 6PU=$1,980). So 12 PU = $2,664 (2022) / $3,300 (2023) / $3,960 (now, computed, [verify]). 20 PU = $5,500 (2023) / $6,600 now.
- Before 1 Jan 2021 the fine was A$444; sixfold rise to 12 PU. https://nationals.org.au/?p=8696 [SOLID]
- Court maximum for false/misleading info (s532(1), s533(1)): 600 PU (~A$198,000 at $330) https://www.agriculture.gov.au/.../infringement-notices-at-the-airport [SOLID-ish]. Civil penalty 60 PU knowingly false info (explanatory stmt https://legislation.gov.au/F2020L01630/asmade/2020-12-17/es/1/pdf).
- "5 years prison" / "10 years, $420,000": only secondary (Deakin factsheet) [DISPUTED]. Safe line: "serious cases can go to court, fines up to ~A$200k, criminal charges possible". Do NOT state prison term.
- Visa cancellation for non-citizens possible (6 cancellations in 2022; Spanish man 2023) [SOLID].

## 4. NEW ZEALAND
- Biosecurity Act 1993. Failing to declare / false declaration (s154N(21)): NZ$400 instant infringement fee, no conviction, strict liability. https://www.mpi.govt.nz/travel-and-recreation/arriving-in-new-zealand/items-to-declare/failing-to-declare [SOLID]
- Deliberate smuggling: court, max fine up to NZ$100,000 (MPI wording) [SOLID]; "5 years prison" appears only in a travel blog [DISPUTED] ; false info to officer: up to 12 months + $50,000 (MPI case text) [1 source].
- Proposed (Oct 2025 draft bill): $400 -> $800 for high-risk (fresh fruit, meat), others stay $400; NOT confirmed enacted. https://www.1news.co.nz/2025/10/29/a-forgotten-apple-in-your-luggage-could-soon-cost-you-more [SOLID as proposal]
- MPI says declare: fruit, veg, eggs, meat, honey, cooking ingredients, herbs, seeds, spices, anything of plant/wood, used outdoor gear incl dirty boots/shoes, tents. Clean boots/camping gear before arrival (no soil/seeds). https://www.mpi.govt.nz/bring-send-to-nz/bringing-and-posting-items-to-nz/travelling-to-new-zealand [SOLID]
- Detection: dogs, x-ray, search of bags (MPI). Amnesty bins: no source found [UNVERIFIED].
- Stats: old (2016) "9,100+ infringements and 1,135 warnings to air passengers in the year" https://www.mpi.govt.nz/news-and-resources/media-releases/more-biosecurity-fines [OLD]; ODT "year's food import fines near $3 million": Auckland 5,192, Queenstown 905, Christchurch 781 fines (year unclear) https://www.odt.co.nz/node/3001178 [DISPUTED year].

## 5. WHY (to confirm/cite in script)
- Island isolation, huge agriculture exports; threats: Queensland/Mediterranean fruit fly (fruit), foot-and-mouth + African swine fever (meat/dairy/pork), varroa/bee disease (honey), seeds/weeds, soil pathogens, timber pests (borers). FMD outbreak in Bali 2022 triggered tougher checks (Darwin cases) [SOLID]. Not independently cited in this pass: add URL if script uses economic figures.

## 6. CHOSEN 7 ITEMS
1 Fresh fruit (apple) - AU declare, 6PU $1,980 / NZ $400 (->$800 proposed); fruit fly. Clean, sealed fruit often still seized.
2 Meat/sausage/ham sandwich - AU 12PU, Perth pancetta, Darwin McMuffin, FMD/ASF; effectively refused.
3 Cheese/dairy - AU declare, same 12PU (goat cheese Perth case); allowed only commercially packed small amounts after inspection (check rule).
4 Honey - AU must declare, inspected; allowed in most states, WA special rules; NZ declare (bees).
5 Seeds/nuts/plant bits/flowers - Brisbane $5,500 seeds, Navya jasmine $1,980, rose; biggest fines.
6 Muddy hiking boots / camping gear - both declare; clean first; soil pathogens/seeds.
7 Wooden souvenirs - AU declare wooden articles (borers); NZ anything of wood declare.
(Alternatives dropped: sports gear, tea/herbs - no verified specifics.)
