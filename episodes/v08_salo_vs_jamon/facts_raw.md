## 1. ASF basics
- [SOLID] ASF is a viral disease of pigs and wild boar; not harmful to humans (thepigsite 2026-02 article states it; WOAH/EFSA say same).
- [SOLID] Spread: wild boar contact, infected pigs, ticks (Africa), and above all humans: contaminated pork products, food waste, vehicles. EFSA: human-mediated "long jumps" create new clusters far from known zones (EFSA epidemiological reports; pigprogress "EFSA: Humans play a major role").
- [SOLID] Virus is genotype II since Georgia 2007 (first detected June 2007; suspected cause: illegal disposal of contaminated food waste). Poland/Baltics 2014. Germany first case Sept 2020. (3tres3 / UK APHA / tcrh.de timelines.)
- [DISPUTED] Ukraine "2012": one source says ASF reached Ukraine 2012 (via Russia 2007->Ukraine 2012->Belarus 2013); not confirmed by a primary source here. CHECK DPSS/WOAH before using.
- Survival in cured pork (lab data):
  - [SOLID] Italy study (Prev Vet Med 2019, pubmed 30621891): live virus found up to 18 d (salami), 60 d (pork belly), 83 d (loin) of curing.
  - [SOLID] Mebus et al 1993 (Food Microbiol, Spanish dry-cured): Serrano ham virus-free by day 140; Iberian ham by day 140; Iberian loin by day 112. Mebus 1997 (Food Chem): ASFV inactivated within commercial Spanish curing times.
  - [UNVERIFIED] "399 days in Parma ham" / "1000 days frozen" / "110 days chilled" - could not retrieve; EFSA 2021 opinion (doi 10.2903/j.efsa.2021.6558) covers feed/bedding/vehicles, not a pork table. Do NOT quote these numbers without checking.
  - [SOLID] EFSA rates salted/dried/fermented meats (salami, dry-cured ham) as moderate transmission risk (EFSA risk assessment presentation); long storage lowers survival.
  - Narrative: virus is tough - lasts months in raw/frozen meat, weeks-months in curing; key reason cured ham is rated lower risk but not zero.

## 2. Spain 2025-26 outbreak (big hook: "the jamon country got ASF")
- [SOLID] 28 Nov 2025: ASF confirmed in 2 dead wild boar, Cerdanyola del Vallès / Collserola hills near Barcelona; first ASF in Spain since 1994. (Ministry of Agriculture; catalannews; WOAH webinar https://rr-europe.woah.org/?p=20763)
- [SOLID] WOAH webinar 22 Dec 2025: 27 positive of 193 carcasses tested (19 Dec); infected zone 20 km radius, 95 municipalities; all 57 pig farms in zone negative; genotype II group 29, related to Georgia 2007 but differs by ~30 mutations, unlike EU circulating strains.
- [SOLID] Feb 2026: 155 positive boar, first cases outside initial zone (El Papiol, Molins de Rei) (thepigsite 2026-02-13). End of March 2026: 232 positive boar; ~24,000 animals culled (arabnews/other; check). No domestic pig farm cases reported in sources found.
- [DISPUTED] Origin: possible leak from IRTA-CReSA BSL3 lab <1 km from first carcasses (Georgia 2007 is a lab reference strain). Spanish ministry report "not conclusive", police probe; EU experts (11-12 Dec) found no obvious escape route; lab genome study found no matching mutations. Alternative: infected meat/sandwich discarded by traveller (officials mentioned this too). NO final ruling found. Treat as unresolved.
- [SOLID] Trade: Non-EU exports of Spanish pork largely paused/limited; China restricted only Barcelona province; Japan, Mexico, Taiwan broader bans; about 1/3 of export certificates blocked at one point (Reuters via arabnews/scmp). Intra-EU trade mostly continued except the 20 km zone. Farm group Unión de Uniones: >EUR 600m lost by ~Apr 2026; earlier estimate EUR 24m/week. [Figures from news, medium reliability.]
- [UNVERIFIED] Specific effect on jamon exports during outbreak - not found. Pre-outbreak: cured ham exports 2024 = 60,846 t / EUR 735m (Serrano Consortium via euromeatnews); 2025 record ~73,000 t / EUR 856m (foodswinesfromspain, check).
- Status after ~Apr 2026 NOT verified (searches returned nothing newer). Check MAPA / WOAH before scripting "latest".

## 4. EU rules
- [SOLID] Travellers from non-EU countries may NOT bring meat or dairy for personal use (Reg (EU) 2019/2122 + 2021 Delegated reg; Commission page https://food.ec.europa.eu/animals/animal-products-movements/personal-imports_en ; your-europe https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/carry/meat-dairy-animal/ ). Exceptions: baby milk/food, medical special food (small amounts). Seized goods are destroyed as category-3 waste (practice; exact EU text not retrieved) [UNVERIFIED wording]. Salo is pork product -> banned for personal import; Germany customs: personal items must meet same vet rules as commercial.
- [SOLID] Commercial import of pork from Ukraine into EU is NOT open as of 2026 sources: June 2025 SSUFSCP/DG SANTE agreed to start accreditation for beef, pork, eggs; EU 2026 audit programme lists Ukraine for pork animal health and heat-treated pork products (agrinfo.eu; interfax.com.ua; mind.ua). No source found showing approved Ukrainian pork establishments for EU. Ukraine is in Annex I of Reg 2021/405 (residue controls, DG SANTE audit Sep-Oct 2025, dpss.gov.ua) - this is general listing, not pork approval. Ukraine pork exports 2025 only ~2,200 t (-30%), main buyer UAE (pigua.info MeatForum 2026).
- [SOLID] Reverse: Ukraine recognised regionalisation (2025); Spain re-gained Ukraine market access with conditions (no ASF within 10 km of establishment for 40 days before slaughter) - ukragroconsult/3tres3. Irony hook: Ukraine had closed to Spanish pork as ASF fear.
- [SOLID] Regulation (EU) 2023/594 sets ASF restricted zones I/II/III; products from zones need risk-mitigating treatment (heat treatment, or for raw cured ham maturation >= 9 months, aw <=0.93, pH <=6.0 per UK OSTA note summarising EU rule). Italian PDO hams (Parma, San Daniele) 400-day curing widely used. [Exact Annex conditions: check EUR-Lex https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2023/594/oj ; I could not read Annex.]
- Key logic for script: EU treats each region by risk. Intra-EU trade uses zones + treatment + registered plants + traceability; a traveller's salo has none of these (unknown origin, unknown farm, no heat/cure standard, no certificate). Salo is raw salted fat, often short-salted, not heat treated, not a 12-48 month process.
- [UNVERIFIED] EU approved imports of cured ham from specific non-EU countries (US/Brazil/China) - not found. Only mention if checked.
