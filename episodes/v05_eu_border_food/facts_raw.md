# v05 EU border food (UA -> EU/Poland) — raw facts (researched 2026-11-16)
Tags: [SOLID] = confirmed on official/gov source. [DISPUTED] = sources conflict / only secondary / unverified.
Note: several official pages returned 429/403 to fetch; EU Commission page was read via the working URL below.

## Legal frame
- Meat, milk and their products: personal import from non-EU countries into EU is banned. Basis: Delegated Reg (EU) 2019/2122 (replaced Reg 206/2009), applied via Reg (EU) 2017/625 controls. [SOLID]
  https://food.ec.europa.eu/animals/animal-products-movements/personal-imports_en
- Exceptions (per traveller, non-refrigerated, sealed retail packs, unopened unless in use): infant milk powder/infant food/medical special food/medical pet food up to 2 kg [SOLID]; fishery products (fresh/dried/cooked/salted/smoked fish, shrimp, lobster) up to 20 kg OR one whole fish if heavier [SOLID]; other animal products (honey, live oysters/mussels, snails) up to 2 kg [SOLID]. Same Commission page + https://zoll.de/EN/Private-individuals/Postal_consignments_internet_order/Shipments-from-a-non-EU-country/Restrictions/Goods/Animals-and-plants-products-containing-animal-or-vegetable-substances/Protection-against-animal-diseases/protection-against-animal-diseases_node.html
- Not applying: intra-EU movement; products from Andorra, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, San Marino, Switzerland; Faroe/Greenland 10 kg (Poland KAS source says Faroe/Greenland/Iceland 10 kg). Ukraine NOT in any exemption list. [SOLID]
- Bigger amounts: treated as commercial import — vet certificate + presentation at border post with vet control (CHED). [SOLID]
- Reason: risk of introducing epidemic animal diseases (ASF, foot-and-mouth, avian flu). zoll.de: introduction "cannot be excluded". [SOLID]
- Commission's "Bringing food products to the EU" page does NOT cover eggs, plants, ASF, fine amounts (it says so). Reg 2019/2124 (transit/ traveller controls) not verified — do not cite it in video.
- Food bought in EU, taken out, then brought back from non-EU is also banned (Polish KAS/IAS Szczecin via infor.pl). [SOLID-secondary]
  https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/wiadomosci/5944116,do-ue-nie-mozna-przywozic-zywnosci-pochodzenia-zwierzecego-dlaczego-jakie-sa-wyjatki.html
- Ban also applies to post/courier parcels (same source).

## Allowances (Polish MF page, archived 2012 but figures match EU standard) https://finanse-arch.mf.gov.pl/web/wp/information-for-travellers
- Alcohol (adult, over 17 per page): spirits >22% = 1 L; drinks up to 22% = 2 L; still wine 4 L; beer 16 L. Can mix by percentage. [SOLID figures; verify current on podatki.gov.pl]
- Goods value duty-free: land EUR 300, air/sea EUR 430 (consumer). Tobacco land: 40 cigarettes. (skip tobacco in video)
- Homemade vodka/samogon: counts as spirits; same 1 L limit [SOLID by logic]; unlabeled bottle -> hard to prove strength; cheap trap for story. [DISPUTED: no explicit official line on samogon]

## Ukraine-specific
- No special "Ukraine lane" for personal food: Ukraine = third country, land border controls at Ukraine/Belarus/Russia crossings (infor.pl above). [SOLID]
- EU-Ukraine trade deals (ATM/DCFTA quotas) and Polish 2023 embargo on Ukrainian farm goods concern COMMERCIAL goods. KAS stated Poland's April 2023 ban did not apply to travellers' personal goods (expired 30.06.2023). [SOLID-secondary] https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/wiadomosci/5727465,kas-zakaz-przywozu-produktow-rolnych-z-ukrainy-nie-dotyczy-podroznych.html
- Temporary protection status does NOT change food import rules. [SOLID by logic; no source says otherwise]
- 2024-2026: no found change to personal-import food rules. Border blockades by Polish farmers/hauliers 2025 irrelevant to travellers. Poland installed X-ray scanners at Ukraine crossing (auto-swiat.pl). [DISPUTED date/detail]
- KAS/SG 2026 seizures at Medyka: unidentified mushrooms, 34 kg fly agaric (not food rule, but vivid). [SOLID, strazgraniczna.pl]

## Enforcement / consequences
- Non-compliant food: seized and destroyed on the spot / in special containers at crossing. [SOLID]
- Failure to declare: "fine or criminal proceedings" (Polish tax admin Szczecin). Amounts NOT found officially. Ukrainian media quote 50-1000 EUR / several hundred PLN [DISPUTED - do not state amount; say "mandat or case"].
- Rzeczpospolita 2016 (MF data): Lublin region customs seized ~100 tonnes of food in 2016, mostly cold-cut sandwiches; Hrebenne alone ~40 t (sandwiches, cold cuts, canned goods, cheese); peak late summer/early autumn; seasonal workers and students with homemade preserves. ## THE 7 ITEMS
1. Сало / ковбаса / будь-яка свинина (pork, cold cuts, sandwiches with ham)
   Rule: meat products banned, no quantity allowed (even 1 sandwich). Reason: ASF, virus survives in cured pork for months; wild boar eat discarded food at parking lots (infor.pl). Allowed: nothing; only commercial import with certificate. [SOLID]
   Fact: ~100 t seized in Lublin region in one year, mostly sandwiches (rp.pl 2016).
2. Домашній сир, сметана, масло, йогурт (dairy)
   Banned, any amount, incl. shop-bought sealed. Reason: foot-and-mouth/brucellosis risk. Allowed: powdered infant milk/medical food <=2 kg. [SOLID]
   Fact: even sealed cheese from a Ukrainian shop counts; pasta/bakery containing meat or milk also covered (infor.pl).
3. Молоко, дитяче харчування (milk, baby formula)
   Liquid milk banned; infant milk powder/baby food/medical special food OK up to 2 kg per traveller, sealed retail pack, no fridge needed. [SOLID] Opened packs not OK unless in current use.
4. Мед (honey)
   Allowed up to 2 kg (other animal products). Myth "honey is banned" = FALSE. >2 kg needs commercial rules. [SOLID] Also covers snails/live oysters 2 kg.
5. Риба, ікра, сушена риба/ vobla (fish, caviar)
   Allowed up to 20 kg or one whole fish, fresh/dried/smoked/salted/cooked, shellfish dead. [SOLID] Surprising contrast: fish is the ONLY big-allowance animal food. Caviar: fishery product -> within 20 kg; separate CITES paperwork for sturgeon caviar (>125 g personal limit, CITES) [DISPUTED, unverified here — avoid number in video].
6. Яйця (eggs) — homemade/village eggs
   Eggs = animal product, not meat/milk; Commission page silent; likely falls in "other animal products" 2 kg?? [DISPUTED] Safest line for video: not clearly allowed, border vets treat as animal products; don't pack them. Also raw egg products. Don't claim a specific limit.
7. Картопля, фрукти, овочі, гриби (potatoes, fresh produce, home preserves)
   Plant health rules separate (Reg 2016/2031, Impl. Reg 2019/2072): plants/plant products in luggage need phytosanitary certificate in principle; potatoes (edible/seed) from third countries listed as prohibited (Slovenia source citing 2019/2072) vs French customs 2026 listing "potatoes for consumption" as OK — conflict. [DISPUTED]
   French customs (Jan 2026): bananas, durians, coconuts, pineapples, dates free; other fresh fruit/veg subject to certificate. https://www.douane.gouv.fr/en/actualites/traveling-abroad-what-food-can-you-carry-your-suitcase
   Myth "all fruit banned" = overstated; real practice: small personal amounts of fruit often waved through but formally need certificate; potatoes = the official no. Home preserves with meat (tushonka, lard jars) = meat rule (banned); pure veg jars = grey zone.
   Fact: seasonal workers & students carry jars/lard; Poland also seized 34 kg wild mushrooms (2026) - mushrooms are plant products.
   (Alcohol dropped from 7 as user asked about it only as limits; put limits as bonus segment: 1 L spirits / 4 L wine / 16 L beer.)

## Myths to avoid in script
- "Honey is banned" — false, 2 kg ok.
- "All fruit/veg banned" — overstated; potatoes are the official ban, produce needs certificate formally.
- "Only large amounts are banned" — false for meat/dairy: ANY amount.
- "Sealed shop-bought sausage is fine" — false.
- "Ukrainians with temporary protection have exemptions" — false.
- "Poland's 2023 farm embargo applies to your bag" — false (commercial goods).
- Don't state fine amounts as fact; don't cite 2019/2124.

## Genuinely allowed (summary)
Fish & fish products up to 20 kg; honey up to 2 kg; infant milk powder/baby food/medical foods up to 2 kg; bread, sweets, chocolate, cereal, tea, coffee, pure bakery without meat/milk (plant/non-animal foods — Reg 2019/1793 flag; not verified here, [DISPUTED] for limits); alcohol 1 L spirits / 4 L wine / 16 L beer (check current KAS). Vegan-type shelf foods generally OK.
